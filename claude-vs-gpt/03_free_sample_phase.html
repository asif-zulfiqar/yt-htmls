The Free Sample Phase
Land grab → habit forms → competition thins → price reset. Not a conspiracy — just the playbook of every platform that ever dominated a market.
WE ARE HERE
PHASE 01
Land Grab
Give away cheap or free access. Build the user base. Hook developers before they realize they're dependent.
PHASE 02
Habit Forms
Users can't work without it. If the API goes down, work stops. The tool becomes infrastructure — not just software.
PHASE 03
Competition Thins
Alternatives lose ground. Switching costs rise. Market consolidates around 1-2 winners. Data moat is fully built.
PHASE 04
Price Reset
Costs go up. Limits tighten. The habit means you pay anyway. The $200/month was the teaser, not the real price.
